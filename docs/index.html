Engineered for Power Users

Everything you need to automate your desk and workspace without bogging down your system.

⚡ Instant Event Engine

Zero polling loops. Kernel vnode file watchers and Darwin notifications stream Focus mode, frontmost apps, and display changes to Home Assistant the millisecond they happen.

🎙️ Audio & Camera Privacy

Instantly trigger "On Air" studio lights or office busy signs when your webcam turns green or your microphone stream begins.

🔋 Power & Battery Telemetry

Monitors battery percentage, state, and AC/battery power transitions via low-level IOKit runloop sources.

🎛️ Remote Control Endpoints

Put your Mac to sleep, lock the screen, or mute system audio right from Home Assistant automation triggers or dashboard cards.

🛡️ Privacy-First Entity Pruning

Uncheck any sensor in the Preferences tab, and Mac2MQTT automatically publishes an empty discovery payload, removing the entity entirely from Home Assistant.

🚀 Clean Menu Bar Citizen

Runs as an accessory background process. No Dock icon, no accidental Cmd+Tab switching, and launches silently at login.

Get Started in 60 Seconds

Simple, friction-free installation.

1

Install App

Download Mac2MQTT.dmg, open it, and drag Mac2MQTT into your Applications folder.

2

Configure Broker

Click the antenna icon in your menu bar, open Preferences, and enter your MQTT broker IP and credentials.

3

Automate!

Your Mac appears instantly in Home Assistant under Settings → Devices & Services → MQTT.

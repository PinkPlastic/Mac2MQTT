Installation

Get up and running in three simple steps.

1

Install App

Download StateBeacon.dmg, open the disk image, and drag StateBeacon into your Applications folder.

2

Configure Broker

Click the antenna icon in the menu bar, open Preferences, and enter your MQTT broker IP and credentials.

3

Automatic Discovery

Your Mac appears in Home Assistant under Settings → Devices & Services → MQTT.
